# 🏛️ Blue Prince Helpers Hub

**Master the Mansion. Track your Progress. Solve the Mystery.**

`Blue Prince Helpers Hub` is a comprehensive, fan-made standalone helper tool designed specifically for the architectural puzzle game **Blue Prince**. Whether you're navigating the shifting halls of Mt. Hebron for the first time or you're a seasoned explorer looking to optimize your runs, this hub is your ultimate companion.

---

## Why use the Helpers Hub?

In *Blue Prince*, every day brings a new layout, new items, and new challenges. Keeping track of your current run while remembering your permanent progress can be overwhelming. This tool brings everything together in one sleek, interactive interface.

### Key Features

- **Interactive Map Builder:** Place room tiles manually as you explore. Rotate them to match your exact layout and visualize your floorplan in real-time.
- **Dynamic Checklist System:** A "source of truth" for your run. Track found rooms, items, and permanent changes.
- **Smart Synchronization:** The map and checklist are linked. Support slots for Outer Rooms, Blessings, and Coat Checks unlock automatically as you mark progress in your checklist.
- **Run & Effect Tracking:** Monitor your active effects (Gems, Gold, Ivory Dice, etc.) and keep detailed notes on your current board state.
- **Intelligent Suggestions:** Stop guessing names. The tool provides automatic suggestions while you type room or item names.
- **Android Support:** A dedicated Android APK is provided for a seamless experience on your phone or tablet.
- **Optional Cloud Sync:** Power users can enable Supabase integration to sync data across devices. Don't want it? No problem—the tool works perfectly in Local-First mode.

---

## Quick Start

1. **Web:** Simply open `blue-prince-helpers-hub.html` in any modern web browser.
2. **Android:** Install the provided `.apk` file on your Android device to use the hub as a dedicated app.
3. **Usage:**
   - Start by filling out your Checklist as you find things in the game.
   - Use the Map tab to reconstruct your current floorplan.
   - Track your Items and Effects in the right-hand panel during your run.

---

## The Flow: Checklist ➡️ Map

The Helpers Hub is built around a logical flow that mirrors the game's progression:

1. **Discover:** You find a permanent path or a specific room type in-game.
2. **Document:** Mark it in the Checklist.
3. **Unlock:** The corresponding UI elements (like the Outer Room marker or Blessing slots) appear in the Run view.
4. **Strategize:** Place your Trunk markers directly on the map to remember where you left your loot.
